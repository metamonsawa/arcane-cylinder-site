Privacy Policy

Liu Chi-Hsiang (the “Operator”) handles your information in the game “Arcane Cylinder” (the “Game”) as follows.

1. Information Collected

When you play as a guest (playing only on your device), game data is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Operator’s servers. The only information sent to the Operator’s servers is your device’s platform (such as iOS), when checking whether a new version is available. Even when you play as a guest, the information described in the advertising section (3) and inquiry form section (5) is sent to Google as described in those sections.

When you link an Apple or Google account, the following information is stored on the Operator’s servers.

  • Player ID (a number issued by the Operator)
  • Linked account identifier (a number issued by Apple or Google, stored in the Operator’s database after being converted into a form that cannot be reversed)
  • Email address received from Apple or Google during account linking (stored only within the authentication system, not in the game database)
  • Device identifier (a number generated by the Game) and device type (such as iPhone or iPad)
  • Save data (game progress and settings)
  • Purchase records (transaction numbers, products, purchase dates and times, and changes in Pure Magic Stone balances) and records of items received through the inbox

The Operator does not collect your name, address, telephone number, or payment card number (except information you choose to include in an inquiry). Payments are handled by the Store (such as the App Store), and the Operator does not receive your card information.

2. Purposes of Use

The information collected is used for the following purposes.

  • To provide the Game and save and transfer your save data
  • To verify purchases and deliver purchased items
  • To prevent unauthorized use
  • To respond to inquiries
  • To fix bugs and improve the Game

3. Advertising

The Game displays advertisements through Google’s advertising service (AdMob). For advertising purposes, Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, and records of ad displays and interactions.

  • Advertisements tailored to you (personalized ads) are displayed only with your consent. You can choose whether to consent in the initial consent dialog in the Game. You can change your choice later through the Game’s settings.
  • On iPhone, the advertising identifier is used only if you grant permission through Apple’s app tracking permission prompt.
  • If you do not consent, advertisements that are not personalized to you are displayed.

For information about how Google handles information, please see Google’s Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

4. External Services

The Game uses the following services.

  • Amazon Web Services (servers in the Tokyo Region, Japan): Used to host the Operator’s servers and databases. The Operator entrusts data handling to this service provider, and the data is stored in Japan.
  • Apple Inc. (United States): Used for sign-in and purchases through the App Store.
  • Google LLC (United States): Used for sign-in, advertising (AdMob), and inquiry forms (Google Forms).

Apple and Google receive information directly from you under their respective terms and privacy policies. Information about the personal information protection system in the United States is available on the Personal Information Protection Commission’s website.
The Operator does not provide collected information to third parties without your consent, except as permitted by law.

5. Inquiry Form

Inquiries are accepted through Google Forms. When you open the form from the Game, your Player ID, Game version, device model, and OS version are prefilled. You can choose whether to submit the form and can remove the prefilled information before submitting it. If you would like a reply, please enter an email address where you can receive it. Your inquiry and email address are used only to respond to your inquiry and improve the Game.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Information associated with a linked account is retained while the account remains active.
  • You may request account deletion through the Game’s settings. Save data and account linking information are deleted within 30 days of your request. They are deleted from backups within a further 35 days at most.
  • Purchase records are retained for seven years after account deletion for accounting and fraud prevention purposes. These records remain associated only with your Player ID, and their association with your linked account is removed.
  • Server operation records (logs) are deleted after 14 days.
  • Guest data can be deleted using “Delete data on this device” in the Game’s settings.

7. Security Measures

The Operator encrypts communications and stored data to prevent information leaks, loss, and damage.

8. Requests for Disclosure, Correction, and Deletion

You may request disclosure, correction, deletion, or cessation of use of your information held by the Operator. Please contact the inquiry channel (https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScNTBPuTUQzuK9Jbjy7NN0qr8mpKDvpuHxW-K3NU5ORfMLNiQ/viewform?hl=en). After verifying your identity, the Operator will respond in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children’s Information

The Game does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If the Operator learns that information has been collected from a child under 13, that information will be deleted.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is changed, notice of the changes will be provided within the Game.

11. Language

This policy is prepared in Japanese and English, and both versions are official texts. If their meanings differ, the Japanese version prevails.
